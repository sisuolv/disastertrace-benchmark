# Third-party inputs in this private research update

Native NWS METAR and TAF reports are retrieved through Iowa Environmental Mesonet
(IEM). IEM is the delivery service, not an additional independent weather source.
Raw products retain station, issue, validity and provenance information. The
historical-availability scenario is explicitly declared; it is not a first-publication
certificate. The new calendar has no severe-visibility positives at the 1 km threshold.

Qwen3-8B generation uses the existing pinned local model. Captures, version receipts
and the replay implementation are included, without model weights or installed
third-party runtime environments. No account credentials or signed download links
are part of this publication. Prior publication notices continue to apply to the
unchanged EUPPBench/DWD scalar samples and other previously published inputs.

The destination remains the existing private research repository. Technical access,
research interpretation and the terms for any later public dataset release remain
separate; this update does not assert blanket redistribution rights for all candidate
datasets in the sixteen-hazard roadmap.
